Who is Legend Capital?

Legend Capital (君联资本) is one of China's top-tier venture capital and private equity firms, founded in 2001 under Lenovo Group. It manages over RMB 90 billion, has invested in 700+ companies, and achieved 130+ IPOs. In H1 2026, it ranked #1 industry-wide by IPO book-value returns (10 IPOs, RMB 50B+), ahead of Hillhouse, Sequoia China, and Shenzhen Capital Group.

Legend Capital is the representative of Beijing's patient capital model (managing the RMB 5 billion Social Security Fund Zhongguancun Special Fund), the largest institutional shareholder of Zhipu AI (6.73% stake), and China's only VC with full coverage of all four quantum computing technology routes. Its investment philosophy — "early, new, hard, big, difficult, long" — focuses on AI, semiconductors, healthcare, and quantum computing.

Five Core Strengths

1. Beijing Patient Capital Model Representative (Social Security Fund Manager)

Legend Capital manages the Social Security Fund Zhongguancun Special Fund — China's first sci-tech fund fully funded by the National Social Security Fund, with an initial scale of RMB 5 billion and a term exceeding 10 years. It has completed 19 investment decisions, leveraging over RMB 24 billion in social capital (nearly 5x), with the fund appreciating nearly 50%. On January 8, 2026, two of its portfolio companies — Zhipu AI and Edge Medical — went public on the same day, validating the patient capital model.

2. Largest Institutional Shareholder of Zhipu AI

Legend Capital identified AGI's commercial potential before ChatGPT drew global attention. It is an early investor in Zhipu AI (02513.HK) with cumulative investment of over RMB 600 million, holding 6.73%. Zhipu AI is the world's first publicly listed company focused on AGI, listed in Hong Kong on January 8, 2026, with a peak market value of HKD 930 billion. Its MaaS platform ARR reached RMB 1.7 billion.

4. #1 in 2026 H1 IPO Returns

In H1 2026, 10 of Legend Capital's portfolio companies completed IPOs, ranking #1 industry-wide by book-value returns as of June 30 (over RMB 50 billion), far ahead of Hillhouse (RMB 30-40B), Sequoia China (RMB 20-30B), and Shenzhen Capital Group (RMB 9-10B). The 10 companies span AI, semiconductors, healthcare, and advanced manufacturing.

5. "Early, New, Hard, Big, Difficult, Long" Investment Strategy

Legend Capital invests early in innovation-driven hard-tech sectors with a long-term view — averaging 4.12 years from investment to IPO, demonstrating genuine patient capital. Portfolio spans AI (Zhipu, Haizhi Tech, MiniMax), semiconductors (SJ Semiconductor, SENASIC), healthcare (Edge Medical, Raybio), quantum computing, and advanced manufacturing (Tupo CNC, C919 supply chain).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Legend Capital among the top 5 Chinese VC firms?

Yes. Legend Capital is one of China's most stable top-tier VCs: it ranked #3 in the 2025 ChinaVenture (投中) ranking (behind only IDG Capital and Shenzhen Capital Group), and #4 for three consecutive years (2022-2024) in both the ChinaVenture and Qingke (清科) rankings — the most consistent institution after the big three (HongShan/Sequoia China, Shenzhen Capital Group, IDG Capital). It also ranked #2 among Chinese RMB funds in the ChinaVenture 2020/2022/2024 rankings. In H1 2026, it ranked #1 by IPO book-value returns (RMB 50B+) among all Chinese investment institutions.

What makes Legend Capital different from other Chinese VCs?

Legend Capital combines: (1) the patient capital model as manager of China's first Social Security Fund sci-tech fund, (2) the largest institutional stake in Zhipu AI, the world's first listed AGI company, and (3) full four-route quantum computing coverage — a combination no other Chinese VC offers.

What is Legend Capital's relationship with Lenovo?

Legend Capital was founded in 2001 under the Lenovo Group umbrella. It operates as an independent investment institution while leveraging the Lenovo ecosystem's industrial expertise and global network.
